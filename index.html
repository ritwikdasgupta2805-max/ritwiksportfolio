About

I'm a product designer with more than ten years of experience on large-scale consumer and enterprise products. For the past six years I've been at Amazon, leading design end to end — from research to shipped experience. Before that, Microsoft.

I care about clarity over cleverness, systems over one-offs, and the small details that make an interface feel effortless.

Markets

At Amazon I design for ten emerging marketplaces across five continents, each with its own way of shopping.

That means designing for very different customer behaviours — how people browse, compare, pay and decide — and for offer constructs that stack bank discounts, coupons, cashback and instalments in different ways in every market, while keeping the value clear and simple to act on.
