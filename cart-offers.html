Case study · Amazon

Every saving, right where the basket is.

Bringing offers into the Amazon cart, in two phases: first helping customers discover offers, then letting them apply every offer, bank offers included, without leaving the cart.

Phase two in UAE: the bank offer is applied in the cart, and the total drops to match.

The problem

Customers build their basket in the cart. The offers were somewhere else.

Offers lived on each product page, one product at a time. But many customers, especially in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, add several items and check out from the cart. By the time they were ready to pay, the savings for their whole basket were nowhere in sight.

In our research for the product page, customers in the UAE told us the same thing: they expect to apply coupons and offers in the cart. Country teams in the UAE and Saudi Arabia asked for it for the same reason.

Two phases

First show the savings. Then let customers take them.

01 · Discovery · 8 markets

Savings for the whole basket, in the cart

  • A savings banner under the order total shows what the customer has already saved.
  • Apply offers opens every offer available for the basket.
  • Live in India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, South Africa, Egypt, Mexico and Brazil.

Offers are grouped by how the customer pays, so full payment and No Cost EMI each show their best savings first.

02 · Application · UAE and Saudi Arabia

Apply every offer without leaving the cart

  • The customer’s saved cards, each with its saving and an Apply button.
  • Bank offers, coupons and instalment offers in one place, with what’s already redeemed marked clearly.
  • A running total at the bottom, so customers see the saving before they tap Done.

What we learned in India

The experiment in India failed. That told us where to look next.

Next · Experiment coming soon

Make the way in impossible to miss

  • Hypothesis: the entry point to offers wasn’t visually appealing enough for customers to notice it.
  • Each saving now sits in the order summary, with the total already reduced.
  • A green “₹1,700 saved” banner leads to the offers.
  • A note shows which card the saving is based on.

Outcome

Discovery in eight markets. Application in two.

Offer discovery in the cart launched in India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, South Africa, Egypt, Mexico and Brazil. Applying offers, including bank offers, launched in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, where customers build bigger baskets and mostly check out from the cart. In India, the next experiment tests a more visual way into offers.
